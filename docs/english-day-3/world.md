# Day 3 world: "Checking in at the hotel"

The shared source for every Day 3 module. Every fact a module uses must come from here (or be
added here first), so the modules never contradict each other, Day 1 or Day 2. Theme 1.9
("Checking in at the hotel") in `docs/english-themes-plan.md`, week 1 "Your first trip". Goal of
the day: **give your booking name, check your dates, and ask about the Wi-Fi and breakfast** (A2;
the course plan's goal, booking name and Wi-Fi, plus the dates and breakfast that every check-in has). US English (US voices; "gray",
"elevator", "check-out"). Not a real city, hotel or company.

## Where and when

**Riverton**, the same mid-size US city. **Still Saturday**, the day Sara arrived (Day 2).

- Sara reached **the Park Hotel** (210 Main Street, across from Central Station) at about 2:10 p.m.
  and left her big gray suitcase at the front desk. Rooms are ready from 3 p.m.
- **She checks in at about 5:15 p.m.** In between she had a coffee at the café in Central Station.
  (Professional learners know she also got her phone back from Mike at the taxi stand at 5; that
  part is never needed and never mentioned in Intermediate content.)
- **Dinner with Tom at 7 p.m.** (Day 2): a RideNow picks her up across from the hotel at 6:40.

## What happens (Sara's check-in, about 5:15 p.m.)

1. Sara comes back to the front desk. Daniel remembers her suitcase.
2. She says she has a booking under Demir and spells it. Daniel finds it: three nights, check-out
   Tuesday.
3. He asks for her passport and a card for incidentals, and explains the $50-a-night hold.
4. He gives her two key cards for **room 714** on the seventh floor; the elevators are behind the
   desk, on the left.
5. She asks about the Wi-Fi: free; network ParkHotel-Guest; password mainstreet210, written on the
   key-card holder.
6. She asks if breakfast is included: yes, 7 to 10:30 tomorrow (Sunday), The Corner Café.
7. Her phone is dead and her charger is in the backpack: Daniel lends her a charger.
8. She asks the desk to keep her backpack when it arrives tomorrow morning and call her room.
   Daniel says he'll call room 714.
9. Daniel brings her suitcase from behind the desk. She goes up to change before dinner.
