# "Connected speech" (Module lab): spec v1

Status: **approved by the owner in chat ("ok build as your recommendation") and built (Module lab).** Content reviewed by the English-coach reviewer (fixes applied) and the Persian-localization reviewer.

Owner's decisions (as recommended): tap 1 of 3, no speaking, 10 items. The owner also asked whether this can scale to 30+ daily lessons: yes. The patterns are few (about 15–20 groups), but the sentences are unlimited. The plan is 10 items a day matched to the day's topic, with each pattern group coming back 2–3 times a month. The screens are built once; only the content changes. Lab set first, then days 1–10 after the owner checks the voice.

## Purpose

In everyday English, words join, shrink and lose sounds ("What do you want" → "whaddaya want"). Persian speakers know the words on paper but miss them at native speed. Each item has one reduction to catch.

**How it differs from Shadowing:** Shadowing is a long sentence written word by word (memory). Here each item is short, focused on one reduction, answered by tapping, and the reveal shows how it really sounds.

## Patterns (6) and items (10)

| Pattern | Items |
|---|---|
| gonna · wanna · gotta | I'm gonna call her after lunch. · I've gotta leave at six. |
| "you" joining in | What do ya want for dinner? · Did ya finish the report? |
| linking into a vowel | Can you turn it off, please? · I'll be back in an hour. |
| should've · could've | I should've called you. · We could've taken a taxi. |
| dropped t and d | I dunno, maybe next week. |
| tiny "of" and "and" | Can I get a cup of coffee and a muffin? |

Each item has three written choices that differ in the reduced part (all natural, none ruled out by grammar or meaning alone), the answer's position varies, and a "sounds like" line (e.g. "Can ya tur-ni-doff, please?", showing the American flapped t).

## The flow

1. **Start.** Mira: "In everyday English, words join together and get shorter…". Bottom: "Start".
2. **An item.** The sentence plays by itself at natural speed (no text); three written choices unlock once it has played. Quiet: "▶ Hear it again" (once).
3. **The reveal.** ✓ Right / ✗ Not quite (with what they chose, struck through), "It sounds like" (large), "It's written", ▶ Natural and ▶ Slow (the careful written form), and the pattern's tip. Bottom: "Next".
4. **Result.** "7 of 10 right", ✓/✗ per pattern, and the weakest pattern with its tip and its sentences to replay. Bottom: "Done". Quiet: "Try it again".

## Audio

- US voice "Brian". English voices in the app are slowed to 0.8 for learners; this module asks for 1.3 so the voice speaks at its own native speed (≈1.04). Slow is ≈0.72.
- Reductions come from the spelling the voice reads ("gonna", "gotta", "dunno", "do ya", "did ya"), not from speed alone.
- **To check before going further:** listen to all ten at natural speed. If a line sounds odd, change its spelling and make "sounds like" match what the voice actually says.

## Data and cost

No AI, no recording. Saved in this browser only: taps, replays, position. Ten fixed sentences, cached audio: close to zero cost.
