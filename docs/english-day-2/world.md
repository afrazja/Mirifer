The shared source for every Day 2 module. Every fact a module uses must come from here (or be

## Where we are

**Riverton**, a mid-size US city. **Riverton Airport** is about 20 miles from downtown. **Today is
Saturday.**

- **The Park Hotel**, 210 Main Street, downtown. New: it opened last year, so some drivers don't
  know it yet. It's right **across from Central Station**. Check-in from 3 p.m.; you can leave bags
  at the front desk before that.
- **Central Station**: the main train station, downtown. The Airport Express ends here.
- **The highway (Route 9)** is the fast way from the airport to downtown. **Today it's closed
  because of an accident**, so cars go through downtown streets: slower, more traffic near the
  station.
- **City Museum**, across from Central Park (used for "a new place" practice).

## Four ways from the airport to the Park Hotel (the facts)

| | Taxi | Ride app (**RideNow**) | **Airport Express** train | **Bus 7** (shuttle) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Where you get it | The taxi stand, outside door 3 | The pickup zone, level 2 of the parking garage | Under the terminal; follow the green signs | The bus stop outside door 5 |
| How long | 30 min normally; **about 40 today** (highway closed) | The same as a taxi, plus a 5–10 minute wait | 25 minutes to Central Station, then cross the street | About an hour; many stops |
| Price | About $55, plus a tip | $35–45; more at busy times ("surge") | $9 | $3.50 |
| How to pay | Cash or card (card machines can be slow) | In the app only | Ticket machine: card or cash | Card, or exact change in cash |
| How often | Always waiting | Book in the app | Every 15 minutes; **last train 11:30 p.m.** | Every 30 minutes |
| Good for | Heavy bags, tired, door to door | Cheaper than a taxi, door to door | Fast and cheap; elevators at both stations | The cheapest; slow |
| Not good for | The most expensive | You need data on your phone; surge prices | You walk from the station (2 minutes) | Slow; little space for big bags |

Tipping: in a US taxi or ride app, people usually tip about 15–20%.

## What happens (the day's timeline)

1. **On the plane (before landing):** Tom's voice message: his car broke down, he can't pick Sara
   up; take the Airport Express, it's easy, or a taxi if she's too tired. He'll meet her for dinner
   at 7.
2. **At the airport:** after landing, the airline's baggage desk calls (Andrew): the backpack comes
   tomorrow between 8 and 10 a.m. Sara reads the signs and checks her options (the table above). The train is
   cheap and fast, but her suitcase is heavy and she's tired after a long day. She takes a taxi.
3. **The ride** (the Listen and connect conversation, `ride-v2.md`): Mike doesn't know the hotel;
   Sara says it's across from Central Station; the highway is closed because of an accident, so they
   go through downtown; about 40 minutes unless traffic near the station is bad; cards are fine, but
   have cash ready just in case; dropped off at the entrance (heavy suitcase); $55.
4. **Mike's story** (on the way): his strangest passenger. A man left a wedding cake on the back
   seat; Mike drove back across the city to the wedding with it and arrived just in time; they
   invited him to stay for dinner.
5. **During the ride:** a voice message from the Park Hotel front desk (Daniel): the room will be
   ready at 3 p.m.; she can leave her suitcase at the desk. Sara tells Mike about Tom's car.
6. **Arrival:** the taxi left the airport at about 1:30 p.m. and reaches the hotel at about 2:10. Sara
   pays the $55 fare plus a $10 tip, and leaves her suitcase at the desk.
7. **The surprise:** at about 2:30, Sara realizes she left her phone in the taxi (black, red case,
   a small crack in the top corner; the battery was almost dead, since her charger is in the backpack,
   so it's switched off). She calls Riverton Taxi from the front desk phone (the role-play). Mike had
   already called the office at 2:20: he found it on his back seat. Getting it back is free.
   - Sara's own ending (the role-play's first run, `busy`): Mike is on a long ride across town; she
     meets him at the **taxi stand at Central Station**, across the street, at about 5 p.m., and gets
     her phone back.
     (Or Mike leaves it at the hotel front desk at about 5:30.)
   - The role-play's replay variant (`off-duty`): Mike is off duty until 7 a.m. tomorrow; he meets the
     caller at the hotel entrance at 7 a.m., or leaves the phone at the front desk by 8 a.m.
8. **Evening:** Tom's message about dinner at 7 (his car is still broken). Sara reads RideNow reviews,
   then takes a RideNow to the restaurant; Carlos waits across from the hotel.

## Details used by some modules

- The Park Hotel: 210 Main Street; opened on March 13 last year; email desk@park-riverton.com
  (fictional); its "Getting here" page says a taxi takes 30 to 40 minutes depending on traffic,
  about $55 plus a tip, and that some drivers don't know the hotel yet.
- Riverton Taxi: (402) 555-0173 (555 numbers are fictional). Mike's taxi is car GJ741.
- Mike used to know every hotel, but there are many new ones now; when he doesn't know a place, he
  asks if it's near anything. He tells almost every passenger the wedding-cake story.
- The wedding-cake story: one Saturday Mike picked up the bride's uncle at the airport; the uncle
  rushed into his hotel and left the cake on the back seat; the wedding was at a restaurant across
  the city; Mike remembered its name, drove it there just in time, and was invited to stay for
  dinner: "the best tip I've ever gotten".
- The airport's travel update: Route 9 closed after an accident; buses also go through downtown,
  10+ minutes late; the Airport Express is running normally.
- The airport information desk: restrooms behind it; free luggage carts by every door; you can
  borrow a phone charger there (Day 1).
- RideNow reviews: about $38 from the airport; a driver usually in 5 minutes; almost double at 7
  p.m. on weekend nights; some cancellations.
