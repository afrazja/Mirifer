## The story

Day 1: Sara lands from Istanbul with her big gray suitcase. Her small green backpack went to
Frankfurt; the airline will bring it to her hotel tomorrow morning.

Day 2: Sara takes a taxi from the airport to the Park Hotel on Main Street. The driver doesn't know
the hotel, so she explains it's across from Central Station. The highway is closed because of an
accident, so they go through downtown. She checks she can pay by card; the card machine is slow, so
the driver suggests having some cash ready, just in case. At the hotel she asks to be dropped off at
the entrance, because her suitcase is heavy.

**The day's goal** (en / fa): By the end, you can tell a taxi driver where you're going, explain
where it is, ask how long it takes, and check how to pay. / در پایان می‌توانی به راننده بگویی کجا
می‌روی و آن‌جا کجاست، بپرسی چقدر طول می‌کشد و کرایه را چطور بدهی.

## 1. Listen and connect

Speakers: the driver (Andrew, a new voice, so he never sounds like Day 1's agent) and Sara (Emma).
About 140 words.

| # | Who | Line |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Driver | Hi there. Where to? |
| 1 | Sara | Hi. Could you take me to the Park Hotel, please? It's on Main Street. |
| 2 | Driver | Main Street's pretty long. Is the hotel near anything? |
| 3 | Sara | It's right across from Central Station. |
| 4 | Driver | Oh, OK, I know where that is. |
| 5 | Driver | I was going to take the highway, but it's closed because of an accident. So we'll go through downtown. |
| 6 | Sara | OK. How long does it take to get there? |
| 7 | Driver | About forty minutes, unless the traffic near the station is bad. |
| 8 | Sara | Do you take cards, or is it cash only? |
| 9 | Driver | Cards are fine. But my card machine is a little slow today, so maybe have some cash ready, just in case. |
| 10 | Driver | Here we are, the Park Hotel. |
| 11 | Sara | Thanks. Could you drop me off at the entrance? My suitcase is really heavy. |
| 12 | Driver | Sure, no problem. That's fifty-five dollars. |

**Heard in three parts**, each followed by its questions:

| Part | Lines | Title (en / fa) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–4 | Where to / مقصد |
| 2 | 5–7 | The way / مسیر |
| 3 | 8–12 | Paying and arriving / کرایه و رسیدن |

**The day's link phrases** (highlighted in the conversation at the end): `because of`, `unless`,
`just in case`.

**Mira's line at the end:**
- en: "The driver used three small phrases: `because of` (the reason), `unless` (except if), `just in
  case` (to be ready if something goes wrong). They go by fast, so listen out for them."
- fa: کلیدِ حرف‌های راننده سه عبارت کوتاه بود: `because of` (به خاطرِ)، `unless` (مگر اینکه)، `just in case` (محض احتیاط). این‌ها تند گفته می‌شوند، پس حواست بهشان باشد.

**Connect** (cause → result; one cause at a time, the same four results each time):

| Cause | Result | Proof line |
|---|---|---|
| The driver doesn't know where the Park Hotel is. | Sara tells him it's across from Central Station. | 3 |
| There was an accident on the highway. | They go through downtown. | 5 |
| Sara's suitcase is really heavy. | The driver stops at the entrance. | 11 |

The fourth result, shown with the others: "Sara should have some cash ready." (it's in the ride, but
none of the three causes leads to it).

**Choice questions:**

| Kind (en / fa) | Question | Options (answer in bold) | Proof line |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plan and reality / برنامه و واقعیت | What was the driver going to do? | Go through downtown. / **Take the highway.** / Wait until the highway opens. | 5 |
| Condition / شرط | When will the ride take more than forty minutes? | **If the traffic near the station is bad.** / If the card machine is slow. / If the highway is open. | 7 |
| Just in case / محض احتیاط | Can Sara pay by card? | No, it's cash only. / **Yes, but she should have some cash ready too.** / Only at the hotel. | 9 |

**Order:** part 1 → connect 1 · part 2 → plan and reality, connect 2, condition · part 3 → just in
case, connect 3 → how it all connects. (The plan comes before the change, so the learner never
rejects "take the highway" and then has to pick it.)

## 2. Natural phrases

Five sentences in the order of the ride: **get in → explain where → ask the time → check how to pay
→ get out.** Each carries one chunk that replaces a typical Persian-speaker habit, and one sound to
notice. Every chunk is heard first in the conversation.

| # | Situation (en / fa) | Sentence (chunk in bold) | Persian-speaker habit it replaces | Sound tip (en / fa) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | You get into the taxi. / سوار تاکسی می‌شوی. | **Could you take me to** the Park Hotel, please? | "I want go to Park Hotel." / "Please go Park Hotel." | `Could you` runs together: `ku-ja`. / `Could you` را پیوسته بگو: `d` و `y` به هم می‌چسبند و «ج» می‌شوند، یعنی `ku-ja`. |
| 2 | The driver doesn't know your hotel. / راننده هتلت را بلد نیست. | It's **right across from** Central Station. | "It's in front of the station." (from «روبه‌روی») | `across` starts with a weak "uh": `uh-CROSS-frum`. / `across` با یک «اَ»ی خیلی کوتاه شروع می‌شود و تأکید روی `CROSS` است: `uh-CROSS-frum`. |
| 3 | You want to know how long the ride is. / می‌خواهی بدانی تا آن‌جا چقدر طول می‌کشد. | **How long does it take** to get there? | "How much does it take?" (from «چقدر طول می‌کشه») | `does it` runs together: `duh-zit`. / `does it` را پیوسته بگو: `duh-zit`. |
| 4 | Before you arrive, you check how to pay. / قبل از رسیدن، می‌پرسی کرایه را چطور می‌توانی بدهی. | **Do you take cards**, or is it cash only? | "Do you accept card?" (from «کارت قبول می‌کنید؟») | `Do you` is short and quick: `d'ya`. / `Do you` را کوتاه و سریع بگو: `d'ya`. |
| 5 | You've arrived at the hotel. / به هتل رسیده‌ای. | You can **drop me‿off** here, thanks. | "I get down here." (from «پیاده می‌شم») | `drop me‿off`: link them, `drop-mee-YOFF`. / `drop me off` را پیوسته بگو: بین `me` و `off` یک «ی» کوتاه می‌آید و تأکید روی `OFF` است: `drop-mee-YOFF`. |

**What the word check listens for** (only the chunk, never the accent):
1. could / can / would you (please) take me to
2. across from (with or without "right"); "in front of" doesn't count
3. how long does / will / would / is it (going to) take; "how much does it take" doesn't count
4. do you take (credit / debit) card(s); "accept" doesn't count, it's the habit being replaced
5. drop me / us off; "drop off me" doesn't count

## 3. Say it in English

Four things you want to tell a taxi driver, in spoken Persian. You say it in English, your own way;
the AI checks that each point came across and shows a natural version. #1, #3 and #4 come from the
ride; #2 uses today's chunks for a new place (transfer). All four are heard as "To the taxi driver /
به رانندهٔ تاکسی".

| # | Persian | Points (en / fa) | Natural versions (A2) | Hints (en / fa) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | می‌شه چمدونم رو بذارید صندوق عقب؟ یه کم سنگینه. | you'd like help putting your suitcase in the trunk / می‌خواهی چمدانت را در صندوق عقب بگذارد · it's a little heavy / چمدانت کمی سنگین است | "Could you put my suitcase in the trunk, please? It's a little heavy." · "Can you help me with my suitcase? It's kind of heavy." | trunk / صندوق عقب · heavy / سنگینه |
| 2 | می‌خوام برم موزهٔ شهر. روبه‌روی پارک مرکزیه. | you want to go to the City Museum / می‌خواهی به موزهٔ شهر بروی · it's across from Central Park / موزه روبه‌روی پارک مرکزی است | "Could you take me to the City Museum? It's right across from Central Park." · "The City Museum, please. It's across from Central Park." | take me to / من رو ببرید… · across from / روبه‌روی |
| 3 | می‌شه سر راه جلوی یه خودپرداز نگه دارید؟ می‌خوام محض احتیاط یه کم پول نقد بگیرم. | you'd like to stop at an ATM on the way / می‌خواهی سرِ راه جلوی یک خودپرداز نگه دارد · you want some cash, just in case / می‌خواهی محض احتیاط کمی پول نقد بگیری | "Could we stop at an ATM on the way? I'd like some cash, just in case." · "Can you stop at an ATM on the way, please? I want to get some cash, just in case." | on the way / سرِ راه · just in case / محض احتیاط |
| 4 | همین‌جا پیاده می‌شم، ممنون. می‌شه یه رسید هم بدید؟ | you'd like to get out here / می‌خواهی همین‌جا پیاده شوی · you're asking for a receipt / رسید می‌خواهی | "You can drop me off here, thanks. Could I get a receipt?" · "Here's fine, thanks. Can I have a receipt, please?" | drop me off / پیاده می‌شم · a receipt / رسید |

  "in front of"; #3 is where the learner says a link phrase (`just in case`) themselves.
- Names that may come up, to help speech-to-text: "Park Hotel, Main Street, Central Station, City
  Museum, Central Park."

## Decisions for the owner

1. **One story, so chunks repeat on purpose.** The Listen and connect spec says a conversation may
   echo at most one of the day's phrases. The new method echoes all five (hear → say → use). If you
   approve this draft, that rule is updated for every new day.
2. **Free speaking.** Day 2 has many speaking turns, but all guided (the check-in question is the only
   free answer). Day 1 ended with free speaking ("Tell your story"). Suggestion: week 1 alternates, so
   Day 3 (hotel check-in) ends with free speaking again.
