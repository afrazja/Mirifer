# English Day 2: content for every module (draft)

Status: **being written, for review and owner approval.** The owner asked for Day 2 content for
**all 18 Module lab modules**, to test whether the one-world method can feed every module. Two tracks
(owner's decision): **Starter (A1–A2)** and **Confident (B1+)**.

- `world.md`: the shared world (places, facts, people, timeline). Every module's content comes from it.
- `ride-v2.md`: the reviewed taxi ride (Listen and connect conversation, Natural phrases A2 set,
  Say it in English Starter set), v2.
- One file per group of modules (below), each module written in its own built format.

## The level map (which track each module suits)

| Track | Modules | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Starter (A1–A2) | Minimal pairs · Connected speech · Natural phrases (A2 set) · Persian-speaker traps · Dictation sprint | Recognise or repeat; little own speech. |
| Both (A2–B1) | Say it in English · Listen and connect · Quick-fire questions · Spot the lie · Voice messages | Short answers in your own words; the content sets how hard. |
| Confident (B1+) | Tell your story · Speaking card · 60-second free talk · Listen and retell · Find the missing information · Choose and persuade · Role-play with a surprise · Read, rate, respond · Natural phrases (B1 set) | Build sentences and keep talking. |
