# English Day 2: content for every module (draft v2)

Status: **for owner approval.** The owner asked for Day 2 content for **all 18 Module lab modules**,
to test whether the one-world method can feed every module, and for two versions of the day:
**Intermediate (A2)** and **Professional (B1+)**, five modules each. Written by five writers from one
world, then reviewed by the English-coach (×2), Persian (×2) and learning-UX reviewers; their fixes
are applied.

- `world.md`: the shared world (places, facts, people, timeline). Every module's content comes from it.
- `ride-v2.md`: the reviewed taxi ride (Listen and connect conversation, Natural phrases A2 set,
  Say it in English Intermediate set), v2.
- One file per group of modules (below), each module written in its own built format.

## The two daily lessons (proposal)

| | Intermediate (A2) | Min | Professional (B1+) | Min |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Check-in | Mira's hello and casual question (built) | 2 | The same | 2 |
| 1 | Listen and connect: the taxi ride, Intermediate questions | 4 | Listen and retell: Mike's wedding-cake story | 4 |
| 2 | Natural phrases: A2 set (5 sentences) | 4 | Natural phrases: B1 set (5 sentences) | 4 |
| 3 | Persian-speaker traps: 5 of the 8 | 3 | Say it in English: Professional set, 3 items | 4 |
| 4 | Say it in English: Intermediate set (4 items) | 4 | Choose and persuade: train or taxi for a friend | 4 |
| 5 | Voice messages: Tom and the hotel (2 messages) | 3 | Role-play with a surprise: the phone left in the taxi | 5 |
| Recap | Built | 2 | Built | 2 |
| | **About 22 minutes** | | **About 25 minutes** | |

Both follow **hear → say → use**: new language comes in (listening, then phrases), the learner uses it
with support (traps, Say it in English), then on their own (a voice reply, a persuasion, a phone call).
Minutes are estimates; they're timed with real audio before the owner sees the final numbers.

Every other module's Day 2 content is extra practice in the Module lab, with Day 2 chosen.

## What the method showed

- It works: the story modules (the ride, Spot the lie, the retell, the role-play, the voice messages)
  support each other, and nothing contradicts the world or Day 1.
- Skill drills (minimal pairs, connected speech, dictation) only borrow the world's words. That's fine:
  they train ears, not the story.
- Some modules need small code changes before they can take a day's content (each was built with its
  lab content inside it): the role-play's surprise, prices in dollars for Find the missing information,
  per-text wording in Read rate respond, and the intro lines in Voice messages. Those come in Step 2,
  when each module reads its content from the day file.
- English shown inside Persian lines follows each module's screen: «…» in most modules (backticks
  would show on screen), backticks only where the screen parses them (phrase tips, Mira's line in
  Listen and connect).
