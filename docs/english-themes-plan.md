# English daily lessons: themes (v2, for the owner's choice)

Status: **v2, for the owner to choose.** v1 was reviewed by the English-coach and product reviewers; their points are merged here (see the end).

Each daily lesson has one theme and one goal ("By the end, you can…"). Modules are then written for the theme.

## How the month is built

- **4 weeks of 7 days = 28 days.** Each week has 6 theme days and a **big day** (day 7): one longer situation that brings back the week's phrases. It is never called "Review" on screen. This matches the course plan (`english-practice-architecture.md` §6: a theme family per week, day 7 the hardest).
- **Inside a week:** days 1–5 are easier, day 6 (★) is a harder day in the same family, and day 7 is the big day.
- **One fixed path for everyone** at launch. The onboarding reason is recorded. Starting each learner with "their" week can be tested later, with a few hundred learners.
- **Week 1 samples every reason** (travel, everyday, living abroad, work), so nobody gets four travel days in a row.
- **Level:** the lessons move from A2 to B1 level over the month. Nothing on screen promises a level.
- **Every goal works in the 15-minute lesson**, which is listening, phrases and your turn. The scene with a twist is only in the 20-minute lesson, so no goal depends on it. The big day has a 15-minute version: a longer listening, then Quick-fire on the week's phrases.
- **Sounds** (Minimal pairs, Connected speech) don't depend on the theme. Each week has one sound focus.

## What each theme day needs (the module slots)

1. **Listening:** a short real-life conversation in the theme (Listen and connect, Spot the lie or Dictation).
2. **Phrases:** 5 natural sentences that tell a short theme story (Natural phrases).
3. **Your turn:** the speaking module in the last column (Tell your story, Quick-fire, Speaking card, or Choose and persuade).
4. **20-minute lesson only:** the scene with a twist in the theme.

The optional extra slot (reading or a sound game) is left out until there are retention numbers.

## Care with topics (from the English-coach review)

- **Where you live / home town:** keep prompts on daily life (your street, your routine, shopping). The AI steers away from politics gently and without judging. Home can be painful for people who left.
- **Passport control:** neutral only. No refusal or detention twists; at most "they ask for one more document". Never give visa advice.
- **Food and plans:** don't assume alcohol, pubs or dating. Invitations are neutral (coffee with a colleague). "Does this have pork in it?" can be one natural option, without assuming the learner is religious.
- **Meeting someone new:** "Where are you from? Iran" never gets a negative reaction or a stereotype. Learner characters have Iranian names.
- **Health:** prompts ask about someone else or a trip. Details are never stored.
- **Avoid:** religion, hijab, salary, immigration status.

## Build order

1. **Week 1 (days 2–7)** before inviting the first 30–50 learners. With fewer than 7 days, nobody reaches day 7, so day-7 return can't be measured.
2. **Week 2** while they use week 1.
3. Weeks 3–4 after the first day-7 numbers.

**How we'll know it works:** day-7 return, sessions per learner in days 1–7, and completion per day. A day whose completion drops sharply needs rewriting.

**Later packs (ideas):** a visa or embassy interview (handled with the same care), school and children, emergencies ("Call 112 and say where you are"), online shopping.

## What changed from v1

- **Product review:**
  - 7-day weeks with a big day instead of 5-day units with "Review" days;
  - week 1 samples every reason instead of four travel days;
  - one fixed path at launch;
  - build week 1 before inviting learners;
  - the optional slot is left out for now.
- **English-coach review:**
  - goals never depend on the 20-minute scene;
  - two actions per goal;
  - "Giving your opinion" moved from A2 to week 4 (B1);
  - added passport control, a video call at work, a parcel or bill problem, booking by phone before the doctor, a bank account, neighbours and changing plans;
  - dropped "What's changing" (risky) and "Comparing and choosing" (abstract);
  - the "a mistake" interview question dropped;
  - the speaking module chosen per day;
  - care rules for sensitive topics;
  - no level promise.
- **Exam-style:** one reviewer said keep it as its own unit and the other said fold it in. It is now week 4, together with opinions and stories, so it still serves the exam reason.
