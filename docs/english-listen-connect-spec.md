# "Listen and connect" (replaces "Listen and act"): spec v1

Status: draft for review, then owner approval.

## Why the old module goes

Owner's verdict: "Listen and act" was shallow.
- Three rounds of "put the X in the Y", with the same six objects and five places.
- The only skill was holding a short list in memory.
- The emoji icons were vague and misleading.
- A B1 learner hears nothing new and learns no English from it.

## What B1 learners need from listening

B1 learners catch most single words. They lose the connections between them: which problem goes with which fix, what caused what, who has what, what changed ("instead", "unless", "until"). Real conversations at a desk or on the phone are built from those links.

The goal: **follow how the pieces of a real conversation connect, and notice the small words that carry the links.**

## The module

Module 1 of the day, about 4 minutes. Owner's design rule: one focused thing per screen, one primary button at the bottom, no icons (text only).

1. **Listen** (Mira's instruction on top, as in every module):
   - One realistic conversation, about 45–60 seconds, between two voices (the coach voice and a second English voice). It is new every day and tied to the day's theme.
   - Day 1 is at a lost-luggage desk.
   - Play, at most 2 times. The words are not shown before the questions.
2. **Questions:** four questions, one screen each. Each question asks about a link, never about a single word. Three kinds:
   - **Connect** (one screen, three pairs): tap an item on the left, then its partner on the right. The pair gets a soft colour and a line number; tapping again undoes it. Three left items and four right items (one distractor). Then "Check".
   - **Why / so** (choose one of three): a cause → result question. The wrong options are things said in the conversation, but linked to something else.
   - **What changed** (choose one of three): a plan that changed ("instead", "unless", "until", "only if").
3. **The words that carried it** (one screen):
   - The whole conversation as text, with the link words highlighted (because, so, instead, unless, until, as long as), each with ▶.
   - Under it, one line from Mira about the day's link words. Bottom: Next.

The result after each question (✓, or the right answer) stays on the same screen, then Next. "Listen again" stays available while plays are left. Score: correct answers out of the questions (the Connect screen counts once per pair).

## Day 1 content (lost luggage, theme: a problem while travelling)

Voices: **Agent** (second English voice) and **Sara** (coach voice is not used for Sara, so Mira stays Mira).

> Agent: Hello, how can I help?
> Sara: Hi. I've just arrived from Istanbul, but only one of my two bags came out.
> Agent: I'm sorry. Can you describe the missing one?
> Sara: It's a small green backpack. The big grey suitcase is here, it's fine.
> Agent: Let me check... The backpack was put on the wrong plane, because the label had the wrong flight number. It's in Frankfurt now.
> Sara: Oh no. When can I get it?
> Agent: It will come on the evening flight, so we can deliver it to your hotel tomorrow morning.
> Sara: The problem is, my medicine is in that backpack. I need it tonight.
> Agent: In that case, buy what you need at the pharmacy here. We'll pay you back, as long as you keep the receipt.
> Sara: And my phone charger is in it too.
> Agent: You can borrow one at the information desk instead. Here's your reference number: G-K-four-five-two.

**Q1, Connect** ("Match each thing to what happens to it"):
- The grey suitcase → is already with Sara
- The green backpack → arrives on the evening flight
- The medicine → Sara buys it at the pharmacy
- (distractor) → goes to the information desk

**Q2, Why / so:** Why did the backpack go to Frankfurt?
- ✓ The label had the wrong flight number.
- It was too big for the plane.
- Sara checked in late.

**Q3, Why / so:** The backpack comes on the evening flight. **So** what happens?
- ✓ It's delivered to her hotel tomorrow morning.
- Sara picks it up tonight.
- It goes back to Istanbul.

**Q4, What changed:** What will the airline pay for, and on one condition?
- ✓ The medicine, as long as she keeps the receipt.
- A new charger, if she buys it today.
- A taxi to the hotel, if the bag is late.

**Link words for Day 1:** because, so, in that case, as long as, instead.

Mira's line: "Small words like *because*, *so* and *as long as* tell you how the facts connect. Listen for them, and you'll follow the whole conversation."

## Rules for every future day's conversation

1. 45–60 seconds, two voices, B1. Natural, with one or two everyday reductions ("I've just", "Let me check").
2. It is tied to the day's theme and carries at least four links (cause, result, condition, change of plan).
3. Questions ask about links, never single words. Wrong options are real details from the conversation linked to the wrong thing, so a lucky guess is unlikely.
4. No icons and no pictures: text only.
5. It is written by us and reviewed by the English-coach reviewer. No AI at run time.

## Saved data, review, reload

- In the browser for the day only: the answers and the plays used.
- Back: a read-only view with each question, the learner's answer and the right one, and the transcript with link words highlighted.
- Reload: back at the same question. The plays used are kept.
- Progress: correct / total (listening).

## Cost

Two TTS voices, generated once and cached. No AI and no speech-to-text, so it costs nothing per learner.
