Most of us learned English from books. Speaking is a different skill, and the only way to build it is to speak, a little, every day, and hear how to say it better.

15 to 20 minutes a day

  1. 1
    Listen. Hear a real conversation: at the airport, in a taxi, at a hotel desk.
  2. 2
    Say it. Repeat natural phrases, then say what you need in your own words.
  3. 3
    Talk. Reply to voice messages, or talk to an AI character who has a surprise for you.

Every day continues the same story, so you always want to know what happens next.
