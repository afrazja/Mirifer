# Day 1 world: "Lost luggage at the airport"

The shared source for every Day 1 module. Day 1 was built first, before the one-world method; this
file writes its world down so Days 1–3 tell one story (`docs/english-day-2/world.md`,
`docs/english-day-3/world.md`). Theme 1.6 ("Lost luggage") in `docs/english-themes-plan.md`, week 1
"Your first trip". Goal of the day: **describe your bag and check what happens next.** US English.
Not a real city, airline or company.

## Two versions (owner's decision, as on Days 2–3)

- **Intermediate (A2):** short, guided speaking; recognising and repeating; short answers.
- **Professional (B1+):** own, longer speech; conversations; reasons and stories.

Both versions start with the same conversation (Sara at the baggage desk), because Day 2 begins with
"Remember Sara? Her backpack went to Frankfurt". Day 1 stays the **shortest** day (about 18–20
minutes): it is every learner's first lesson.

## Where and when

**Riverton Airport**, Riverton, a mid-size US city. **Saturday, about 12:40 p.m.** Sara has just
landed on a flight from Istanbul (with a change of planes in Frankfurt). Her taxi leaves the airport
at about 1:30 p.m. (Day 2).

- **The baggage desk** (Lost and found baggage), in the arrivals hall, next to belt 4.
- **The airport pharmacy**, in the arrivals hall, open until 10 p.m.
- **The information desk**, by door 3: restrooms behind it, free luggage carts by every door, phone
  chargers to borrow (Day 1 says so; see "The charger" below).

## What happened to the bag (the facts)

| | |
|---|---|
| Sara's bags | A **big gray suitcase** (it came out on belt 4) and a **small green backpack** (it didn't). |
| Why | The backpack's tag had **the wrong flight number**, so in Frankfurt it went onto another plane and stayed in Frankfurt. It was supposed to come on Sara's flight. |
| Where it is now | In Frankfurt. It comes on **the evening flight** tonight. |
| Delivery | The airline brings it to her hotel **tomorrow morning, between 8 and 10 a.m.** Free. |
| What's in it | Her **medicine** (she needs it tonight), her **phone charger**, a sweater, a book. |
| The medicine | She buys what she needs at the airport pharmacy; **the airline pays her back as long as she keeps the receipt.** |
| The charger | The agent says she can borrow one at the information desk instead of buying one. **They didn't have one that fit her phone**, so by the afternoon her phone is almost dead (Day 2). |
| The paperwork | The agent fills in a report: her name, her hotel (the Park Hotel, 210 Main Street), a phone number, and a description of the bag. She gets a **file reference, RVR-48213**, to check online. |

## People (and their voices)

Only four English voices: **Andrew** (a), **Ava** (b, always Mira, the coach), **Brian** (c),
**Emma** (d). Characters in the same scene never share a voice; Ava is only ever Mira.

| Who | Voice | About |
|---|---|---|
| **Sara Demir** | Emma | From Istanbul, first trip to Riverton. Tired after a long flight, polite, a little worried about her medicine. |
| **The baggage agent** (at the desk; name: **Marcus**) | Brian | Calm and practical. Says what to do, step by step. This is the Day 1 conversation. |
| **The baggage desk on the phone** (another person, later) | Andrew | The airline's caller in Days 2–3 (confirming the delivery time). Not the agent at the desk. |
| **Tom**, Sara's friend in Riverton | Brian | Never in the same scene as the agent. On Day 2 his car breaks down. |
| **The learner ("you")** | – | Also arriving at Riverton Airport, on their own trip. When the learner gives a name, it's **Karimi** (K-A-R-I-M-I), as on Day 3. Personal questions are about the learner's own life. |

## What happens (Sara at the baggage desk)

1. Only one of her bags came out; she goes to the baggage desk.
2. She describes the missing one: a small green backpack; her big gray suitcase is fine.
3. The agent checks: it was supposed to be on her flight but went to Frankfurt (the wrong flight
   number on the tag).
4. It comes on the evening flight; they'll bring it to her hotel tomorrow morning.
5. Her medicine is in it and she needs it tonight: buy it at the pharmacy; the airline pays her
   back as long as she keeps the receipt.
6. Her charger is in it too: borrow one at the information desk instead.

(The conversation in `src/lib/practice/listen-connect.ts` is this scene, already built and kept.)

## Topics for the learner's own speech (personal, never about Sara)

- A time something went wrong on a trip, and what you did (Tell your story).
- What you always keep in your hand luggage, and why.
- How you describe a bag or a suitcase: color, size, what's special about it.
- Waiting at the baggage belt: how it feels when your bag doesn't come.

## Facts that must stay true across Days 1–3

- Sara flew from Istanbul; her small green backpack went to Frankfurt; it's delivered to her hotel
  tomorrow morning, between 8 and 10 a.m.; her big gray suitcase is with her.
- She bought her medicine at the airport pharmacy and keeps the receipt.
- Her charger is in the backpack; the information desk had none that fit her phone.
- Her hotel is the Park Hotel, 210 Main Street, across from Central Station; she takes a taxi at
  about 1:30 p.m. (Day 2).
