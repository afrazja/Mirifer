# English Day 2: "Taking a taxi" (content draft v1)

Status: **draft, for review and owner approval.** First day written with the new method: one
story, and every part's content comes from it. Once approved, this becomes Day 2's content file.

Theme 1.12 in `docs/english-themes-plan.md`, week 1 "Your first trip". Level A2–B1, US English
(the voices are US; Day 1 says "gray"). Session: 15 minutes (the default for everyone).

## The story

Day 1: Sara lands from Istanbul. Her small green backpack went to Frankfurt; the airline will bring
it to her hotel tomorrow morning.

Day 2: Sara takes a taxi from the airport to the Park Hotel on Main Street. The driver doesn't know
the hotel, so she explains it's next to Central Station. The highway is closed because of an
accident, so they go through downtown. She checks she can pay by card; the card machine is slow, so
the driver tells her to have some cash ready, just in case. She gets out at the hotel entrance.

**The day's goal:** you can tell a taxi driver where you're going, explain where it is, ask how
long it takes, and sort out paying.

**The day's link phrases** (highlighted in the conversation at the end): `because of`, `unless`,
`just in case`.

**Mira's line at the end:**
- en: "The ride hung on three small phrases: `because of` (here's the reason), `unless` (only if
  not), `just in case` (to be ready if it happens). They go by fast, so listen out for them."
- fa: «این سفر به سه عبارت کوتاه بستگی داشت: `because of` (به خاطرِ)، `unless` (مگر اینکه)،

## 2. Natural phrases

Five sentences that follow the ride: **get in → ask the time → explain where → check how to pay →
get out.** Each carries one chunk B1 learners understand but rarely say, replacing a typical
Persian-speaker workaround, and one sound to notice. The learner hears every chunk first in the
conversation (part 1).

| # | Situation (en / fa) | Sentence (chunk in bold) | Persian-speaker habit it replaces | Sound tip (en / fa) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | You get into the taxi. / سوار تاکسی می‌شوی. | **Could you take me to** the Park Hotel, please? | "I want go to Park Hotel." / "Please go Park Hotel." | `Could you` joins up: `cou-joo`. / «`Could you` را پیوسته بگو: `cou-joo`.» |
| 2 | You want to know how long the ride is. / می‌خواهی بدانی راه چقدر طول می‌کشد. | **How long does it take** to get there? | "How much time is it?" / "How much does it take?" (from «چقدر») | Stress `LONG`; `does it` is quick: `duz-it`. / «روی `LONG` تأکید کن؛ `does it` را سریع بگو: `duz-it`.» |
| 3 | The driver doesn't know your hotel. / راننده هتلت را نمی‌شناسد. | It's **right next to** Central Station. | "It's near of the station." / "beside of" | In `next to`, the t of next almost disappears: `NEKS-ta`. / «در `next to` صدای `t` در `next` تقریباً شنیده نمی‌شود: `NEKS-ta`.» |
| 4 | Before you arrive, you check how to pay. / قبل از رسیدن می‌پرسی چطور حساب کنی. | **Do you take cards**, or is it cash only? | "Can I pay with card?" | `Do you` is short and quick: `d'ya`. / «`Do you` کوتاه و سریع است: `d'ya`.» |
| 5 | You've arrived. / رسیده‌ای. | You can **drop me‿off** here, thanks. | "I get down here." (from «پیاده می‌شم») | `drop me‿off`: link them, `drop-mee-YOFF`. / «`drop me off` را پیوسته بگو: `drop-mee-YOFF`.» |

**What the word check listens for** (only the chunk, never the accent):
1. could / can / would you (please) take me to
2. how long does / will / would it take
3. next to (with or without "right")
4. do you take / accept cards (or credit cards)
5. drop me / us off

## 3. Say it in English

Four things you want to tell the driver, in spoken Persian. You say it in English, your own way; the
AI checks that each point came across and shows a natural version.

| 3 | To the taxi driver | می‌شه سر راه جلوی یه داروخونه نگه دارید؟ فقط دو دقیقه کار دارم. | you'd like to stop at a pharmacy on the way · you'll only be two minutes | "Could we stop at a pharmacy on the way? I'll only be two minutes." · "Can you stop at a pharmacy on the way, please? It'll only take two minutes." | on the way · I'll only be… |
