# "Spot the lie" (Module lab): spec v1

Status: **approved by the owner in chat ("do your recommendation but statement only in audio") and built (Module lab).** Content reviewed by the English-coach and Persian-localization reviewers; their fixes are applied (speaker names are written in Persian letters in Persian text).

Owner's decisions:

- the talk plays once, plus one replay;
- **the three statements are audio only**: the text appears only at the reveal.

## Purpose

Listening for meaning and detail together, and not falling for traps (a classic real-life and IELTS listening skill). True statements are usually said in different words; the lie usually reuses the talk's own words with one thing changed. At least one true statement in each talk also keeps a phrase from the talk, so "pick the one that sounds like the talk" doesn't work.

## The talks (one trap each)

| Speaker (voice) | Trap | The lie | Proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sara (Emma) | then ("used to") or now | She still works in a bank. | I used to work in a bank… |
| Tom (Andrew) | a changed detail | His train leaves at six thirty. | I get up at six thirty, because my train leaves at seven fifteen. |
| Mike (Brian) | the wrong person | His sister goes running every morning. | My brother… goes running every morning. |
| Nina (Emma) | a flipped "not" | Nina likes the winters in Toronto. | I don't like the winters at all. |
| David (Andrew) | maybe or for sure | His company will open an office in Dubai next year. | My company might open an office… |

Mira's voice (Ava) only reads the statements, as "Statement A: …" so the letter is never heard as the word "a". The lie's position varies.

## The flow (one thing per screen)

1. **Start.** Mira explains in one line. Bottom: "Start".
2. **Listen.** "Talk 2 of 5 · Tom", a big ▶ Listen. The talk plays one sentence after another (a whole talk in one request was too long for the voice service: the owner got "Couldn't play"). A play counts when the first sentence starts; a failed play costs nothing. Then a quiet "▶ Hear it again (1 left)". Bottom: "Next: the three sentences".
3. **Choose.** Mira reads A, B and C one after another; three big cards (A, B, C). Tapping a card plays it again and chooses it. Quiet: "▶ Hear Sara again" if the replay is unused. Bottom: "B is the lie" (unlocks once all three were heard and one is chosen).
4. **Reveal.** ✓/✗, the three statements as text marked true or lie (and "your choice"), "What Nina said" with the proof sentence and ▶ Hear that part, and the trap explained in English or Persian. Bottom: "Next talk".
5. **Result.** "You found 4 of 5 lies", ✓/✗ per trap, and the explanation for each missed one. Bottom: "Done". Quiet: "Try it again".
